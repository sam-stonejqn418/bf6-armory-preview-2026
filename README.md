## Overview

Battlefield 6 Armory Preview provides a browser-based 3D way to explore the Battlefield 6 armory through a modern HTML interface. The experience is centered on visual browsing, allowing users to open the collection quickly and inspect equipment without navigating a complex application.

Weapons and supporting equipment are gathered into one lightweight preview environment. Users can move through weapons, attachments, gadgets, and throwables during the same browser session, making item inspection and comparison more convenient without requiring a full desktop installation.
